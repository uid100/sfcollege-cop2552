Chapter 14 Recursion

 

If you review the course modules, and the course objectives, you may notice that the next chapter exam (4) covers chapters 11, 12, and 15. As you probably guessed, there is (of course!) more to know about using and applying JavaFX concepts that we won't have time to investigate.

Recursion fits into the same category. It's an interesting concept and one that you may well encounter, and probably already have in other languages. I think that it's important enough that it is worth your time to at least browse through the chapter and try out some of the examples. In fact, I have seen recursion used or at least discussed in technical interviews for advanced programming positions. So, although it won't be tested, we've included the publisher's PowerPoint presentations and you have the textbook chapters as optional material to at least be familiar with.

This is also designed to give you more time to focus on writing your project code and prepare for the next exams. Remember that Exam scores aren't "dropped," but if you do have one that is lower and you do well on the final, the final score may cover the earlier exam, in addition to counting on its own. Go back and read the syllabus if you still have questions about that.
